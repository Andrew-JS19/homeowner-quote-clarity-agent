# Disclosures

This project was newly implemented during the contest submission period. The entrant developed the homeowner quote-comparison concept with AI-assisted research, coding, testing, documentation, and browser assistance.

Two local planning documents created during the contest period informed the requirements and architecture. An earlier, separate synthetic construction-closeout demonstration influenced the general emphasis on evidence labels, but its code, fixtures, prompts, branding, and outputs were not incorporated.

All demonstration quotations, names, prices, references, and security-test instructions in this repository are original fictional fixtures. No real homeowner, contractor, or customer data is used.
